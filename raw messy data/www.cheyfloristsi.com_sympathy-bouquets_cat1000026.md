### Sympathy Bouquets FAQs

#### When should I send a sympathy bouquet?  ▸

You can send sympathy flowers right away, before the service, or in the weeks after—support matters at every stage.

#### What flower colors are appropriate for sympathy?  ▸

Soft whites, creams, pastels, and gentle mixed colors are common choices, but any meaningful color can be appropriate.

#### Can you deliver sympathy bouquets to a home or funeral home in Staten Island?  ▸

Often yes. Add the address or service details at checkout for accurate delivery.

#### What should I write on a sympathy card?  ▸

A short message is perfect: “With sympathy,” “Thinking of you,” or “With heartfelt condolences.”
