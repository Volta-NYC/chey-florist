## Pink Wedding Flowers

Pink flowers range from pale Asiatic lilies to vibrant hot pink gerberas and create a feeling of peace and harmony. A wedding table centerpiece might mix pink stock, larkspur and lisianthus with hot pink and light pink roses.

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/pink/pink-left.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/pink/pink-center1.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/pink/pink-right.jpg)

## White Wedding Flowers

White flowers are as pure and clean as a fresh snowfall. Use white roses for a winter wedding, or calla lilies at any time of year. White daisies are sweet in springtime, while white chrysanthemums and orchids can be used all year round.

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/white/white-left.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/white/white-center.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/white/white-right.jpg)

## Orange Wedding Flowers

Orange flowers are perfect for fall ceremonies, and include peach or burnt-orange roses, dazzling dahlias and coral-hued gerberas. Ask for them in centerpieces, accented with tiny pumpkins and a curl of golden ribbon.

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/orange/orange-left.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/orange/orange-center.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/orange/orange-right.jpg)

## Blue Wedding Flowers

Blue flowers include lofty blue irises, puffy hydrangea and striking blue delphinium. Ask for blue blossoms in a bridal bouquet as your 'something blue,' or to cast a cool tone over an outdoor wedding reception.

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/blue/blue-left.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/blue/blue-center.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/blue/blue-right.jpg)

## Yellow Wedding Flowers

Yellow flowers are sunny and bright, and add a cheerful spirit to any celebration. Ask a florist to use yellow lilies, lemon-yellow snapdragons or brilliant yellow sunflowers in the floral designs for your summertime wedding.

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/yellow/yellow-left.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/yellow/yellow-center.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/yellow/yellow-right.jpg)

## Purple Wedding Flowers

Purple flowers are whimsical and unique. The rich purples of dahlias make a fanciful statement during a wedding ceremony, while pale mauve lilacs and lavender roses have an old-fashioned, artistic charm.

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/purple/purple-left.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/purple/purple-center.jpg)

![](https://assets.eflorist.com/images/wedding/flower-colors/purple/purple-right.jpg)
