### Funeral Service Bouquets FAQs

#### What is a funeral service bouquet?  ▸

It’s a respectful arrangement designed to be displayed during a funeral, memorial, or visitation.

#### Can you deliver funeral service bouquets to a funeral home in Staten Island?  ▸

Often yes. Include the funeral home name, service time, and any delivery notes at checkout.

#### When should I order flowers for a service?  ▸

As early as you can for the best selection. If timing is tight, checkout will show the earliest delivery options.

#### What should I write on the card for funeral flowers?  ▸

Keep it sincere and simple: “With deepest sympathy,” “Thinking of you,” or “With heartfelt condolences.”
