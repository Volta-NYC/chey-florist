### Tulips FAQs

#### When are tulips in season?  ▸

Tulips are most popular in spring, though availability can vary
by market and time of year.


#### What do tulips symbolize?  ▸

Tulips often represent cheerful affection, comfort,
and fresh new beginnings.


#### Do you deliver tulip arrangements in Staten Island and nearby areas?  ▸

#### How do I help tulips last longer?  ▸

Keep them in fresh water, trim the stems regularly,
and place them in a cool spot away from direct sunlight.
