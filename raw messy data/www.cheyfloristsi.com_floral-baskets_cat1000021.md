### Floral Baskets FAQs

#### When should I send a sympathy floral basket?  ▸

Anytime—immediately after a loss, before a service, or in the weeks that follow when support still matters.

#### Are floral baskets appropriate to send to the family’s home?  ▸

Yes. Baskets are easy to place and don’t require the recipient to find a vase.
