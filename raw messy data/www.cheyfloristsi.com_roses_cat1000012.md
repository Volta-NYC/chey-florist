### Roses FAQs

#### What do different rose colors mean?  ▸

Red = love, pink = admiration, white = remembrance/sympathy, yellow = friendship, orange = energy and excitement.

#### How long do roses usually last?  ▸

With fresh water and a clean vase, many roses look great for about a week (sometimes longer depending on conditions).

#### Do you offer rose delivery in Staten Island today?  ▸

Delivery availability varies by date and demand. The soonest available delivery options will show during checkout.

#### Can I add a note or gift with my rose order?  ▸

Yes—most orders allow a card message, and additional options may be available at checkout.
