### Fruit & Food Baskets FAQs

#### What is typically included in a fruit and food sympathy basket?  ▸

Contents vary by design, but many include a mix of fresh fruit and comforting snacks that are easy to share.

#### Are fruit and food baskets appropriate to send after a loss?  ▸

Yes. They’re a thoughtful way to support the family—especially when multiple people are visiting or helping.

#### Can you deliver a sympathy basket to a funeral home in Staten Island?  ▸

Often yes. Include the funeral home name, service time, and any delivery notes at checkout.

#### Can I add a card message with my sympathy basket?  ▸

Yes. You can include a heartfelt note during checkout, and we’ll attach it with your gift.
